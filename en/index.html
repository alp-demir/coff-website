In the app

Four tabs, one coffee routine.

Everything in Coff Circle opens from the four tabs at the bottom.

  • Today

    Add today’s coffee: what you had, and whether at home, at the office or at a café. See how many people near you had coffee today and what they chose most.

  • Discover

    See people whose coffee taste, reasons and city are close to yours. Tap Connect on someone you’d like to meet.

  • Connections

    When you both want to connect, a chat opens. Blocking and reporting are inside every chat.

  • Profile

    Your coffee ranking, your favorite café and why you’re here. Photos may be reviewed before they go live.

Not a dating app

Meeting people can start with something you already do every day.

You choose why you’re here on your profile. Discover weighs it together with your coffee taste and your city, and a shared coffee makes the first message easier.

Privacy and safety

You stay in control.

The Coff Circle team does not read your private messages under normal circumstances. If someone reports a conversation for safety reasons, an authorized team member may review limited evidence tied to that report.

  • No one browses chats; only limited evidence from a reported conversation is reviewed.
  • Blocking and reporting are always at hand inside the app.
  • Your exact location is never shown. When you log a coffee, you choose the place (home, office, café).
  • You can delete your account from inside the app.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Coff Circle?

A mobile app for meeting new people through your coffee habits. It works on iPhone and Android.

Is Coff Circle free?

Downloading it and using the core features is free. Some features, like seeing connection invites right away, come with an optional Coff Premium subscription.

When does it launch?

Coff Circle launches in Turkey on the App Store and Google Play together. We will announce the date on this page.

Is it available in English?

Yes. The app is in Turkish and English. It follows your phone’s language, and you can change the language for Coff Circle alone in your phone’s settings.

Are my messages safe?

The team does not read private messages under normal circumstances. When there is a safety report, only limited evidence tied to that report is reviewed.

Is my location shown to others?

No. Location is used to show coffee logs and cafés near you. You choose the place when you log a coffee; your exact location is not shown to other users.
