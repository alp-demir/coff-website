Outside the dating mold

Coff Circle puts meeting people back into everyday life.

Not every new connection has to feel like a big decision. Sometimes loving the same coffee, discovering a new spot in the same neighborhood or sharing where you had coffee today is enough. Coff Circle turns these small but real social signals into a safer and more refined flow.

Privacy and safety

A warm social space, with you in control.

The Coff Circle team does not review your private messages under normal circumstances. If a user reports someone for safety, abuse or inappropriate content, an authorized team member may review limited message evidence related to that report.

  • No live browsing of chats by admins; only limited evidence tied to a report is reviewed.
  • Reporting, blocking and account security tools stay available inside the app.
  • Profile photos may go through moderation before they become visible.
  • You can delete your account inside the app, and the support channel is always open.

Frequently asked

What people ask.

What is Coff Circle?

A mobile app for meeting new people through your coffee habits. It works on iPhone and Android.

Is Coff Circle free?

Downloading it and using the core features is free. Some features, like seeing connection invites right away, come with an optional Coff Premium subscription.

When does it launch?

Coff Circle launches in Turkey on the App Store and Google Play together. We will announce the date on this page.

Is it available in English?

Yes. The app is in Turkish and English. It follows your phone’s language, and you can change the language for Coff Circle alone in your phone’s settings.

Are my messages safe?

The team does not read private messages under normal circumstances. When there is a safety report, only limited evidence tied to that report is reviewed.

Is my location shown to others?

No. Location is used to show coffee logs and cafés near you. You choose the place when you log a coffee; your exact location is not shown to other users.
