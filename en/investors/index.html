Opportunity

We bring meeting people back into daily life.

  • Problem

    In most products, meeting new people has become a high-intent, tiring decision that is cut off from daily life.

  • Why now?

    Local discovery, shared interests and more controlled social experiences are converging on the same need. Coffee culture gives that overlap a familiar ground that repeats every day.

  • Solution

    Coff Circle brings daily coffee logs, coffee activity nearby, social preferences and safety tools together in one mobile app.

Market approach

Density first, then scale.

Coff Circle enters the market not as a broad but shallow social network, but with a focused use case that builds density in cities and coffee spots. The first goal is to turn the daily coffee rhythm of users in Turkey into a strong local network.

  1. Store launch

    Open the core experience in Turkey on the App Store and Google Play together, and validate it with early users.

  2. City-by-city density

    Strengthen local use, retention and the discovery of coffee spots.

  3. Community layers

    Deepen the experience with badges, coffee routes and café discovery.

  4. New markets

    Take the validated model to new cities and countries.

Current stage

Pre-launch.

Coff Circle is getting ready for its first App Store and Google Play release in Turkey, on iPhone and Android. Product flows, safety controls and launch infrastructure are being completed for this first market.
