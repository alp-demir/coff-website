Opportunity

We bring social discovery back into daily life.

01

Problem

In most products, meeting new people has turned into a high-intent, tiring decision that is disconnected from daily life.

02

Why now?

Local discovery, shared interests and more controlled social experiences are converging on the same need. Coffee culture gives this intersection a familiar ground that repeats every day.

03

Our solution

Coff Circle brings daily coffee logs, signals from the area around you, social preferences and safety tools together in one mobile experience.

Market approach

Density first, then scale.

Coff Circle enters the market not as a broad but shallow social network, but with a focused use case that builds density in cities and coffee spots. The first goal is to turn the repeated coffee rhythm of users in Turkey into a strong local network.

  1. 01
    Launch in Turkey on iPhone and AndroidValidate the product experience with early users
  2. 02
    City-by-city densityStrengthen local use, retention and discovery of coffee spots
  3. 03
    New cities and partnershipsExpand a repeatable growth model step by step

Current stage
