KVKK Privacy Notice

This notice gives information about personal data processing in Coff's services under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The Privacy Policy is a separate document and does not replace this notice.

1. Data controller

Your personal data is processed within the Coff service by the natural person Alp Demir, acting as data controller. Coff is not currently operated by a company, so there is no company or MERSIS (Turkish central registry) information.

Public contact channel for KVKK requests: kvkk@coffcircle.com.

2. Categories of personal data processed

Depending on how you use Coff, the following data categories may be processed:

  • Identity, contact and account: first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, a secure hash of your password, account verification and status information.
  • Profile and discovery preferences: city, district, bio, education and work information, interests, availability, social coffee intention, coffee preferences and gender. Coff does not filter discovery by gender, and it does not collect sexual orientation or draw conclusions about it.
  • Photos and user content: profile photo, its storage and processing details, moderation status, messages, check-in notes and content you share in support requests. Uploaded profile photos may go through an automatic pre-screening before they are published; for this, the photo is sent to a content safety service provider. Pre-screening never rejects content directly; it can only send it to review. The final decision on whether a photo is published is made by the authorized team.
  • Location, places and check-ins: when you give device permission, the coordinates used to find nearby places and check the distance, the chosen place, the place source, check-in time and visibility preference. For home, office and other non-café check-ins, the device coordinates are approximated on the server before they are saved.
  • Matching, safety and usage: candidate actions, matches, message times, report and block actions, moderation records and limited product usage events.
  • Subscription and transactions: subscription type and status, product information, transaction identifiers, renewal and cancellation information, and technical reconciliation records. Coff does not receive payment card details directly.
  • Device, session and technical: IP address, device, operating system and app version, notification token, session and security records, errors, crashes and performance information.
  • Traffic data (Law No. 5651): which account used the service from which IP address, on which day and in which time range, and the number of requests.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

Processing is limited to its purposes and based on the following KVKK processing conditions:

  • Account creation, sign-in and account security: to set up and verify the account and provide the Coff service; necessary because it is directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract.
  • Profile, discovery, matching, check-ins and messaging: to run the core product functions the user asks for; necessary because it is directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract.
  • Notifications and support: to deliver requested notifications, solve technical or account problems and answer requests; performance of a contract, a legal obligation where applicable, and the establishment, exercise or protection of a right.
  • Reports, blocking, moderation, fraud and abuse prevention: to protect users and the service; applicable legal obligations, the establishment, exercise or protection of a right, and proportionate legitimate interest, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms.
  • Subscription and financial records: performance of a contract to provide the subscription and reconcile it; a legal obligation for applicable financial and administrative records.
  • Product analytics, error and performance records: to measure the matching and discovery flow, detect problems, improve the service and keep it running safely; proportionate legitimate interest, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms.
  • Keeping traffic data under Law No. 5651: because Coff hosts user content, keeping traffic data for 1 year under Article 5 of that law; it is expressly provided for by law and necessary for the data controller to fulfil a legal obligation.
  • KVKK requests, official requests and disputes: to fulfil legal obligations and to establish, exercise or protect rights.

Device permissions such as location and notifications stay under your control through the operating system. Granting them does not mean you accept this notice, and no separate “I accept” approval is requested for this notice.

4. How personal data is collected

Data is collected electronically through the information you enter in the Coff app or web forms, data provided under device permissions, records created while you use Coff features, server and security logs, support and KVKK communications, and notifications from subscription and technical service providers.

5. Recipient groups and purposes of transfer

Personal data may be transferred to the following recipient groups only to the extent needed for the related service:

  • Technical infrastructure providers: running the server and database, secure content delivery, profile photo storage, sending service emails, and error and performance tracking.
  • App service providers: delivering notifications, verifying subscription and purchase status, and providing map, place search and location-based features.
  • Content safety providers: automatic pre-screening of profile photos before they are published.
  • Authorized public authorities or legally authorized persons: when there is a proper, binding request or a legal obligation.

These providers process data to provide the related infrastructure service, not for their own product advertising. Photos sent for content safety pre-screening are configured so that the provider cannot use them to train its models. Coff does not sell personal data and does not do cross-app ad tracking.

6. Transfers of data abroad

Some of the providers above may have systems or support teams outside Turkey. For this reason, limited personal data needed for hosting, storage, email, error tracking, subscriptions, notifications, content safety pre-screening and map/place services may be transferred abroad or accessed from abroad.

Our current main providers and their countries are: Railway (US company; servers in the European Union, the Netherlands) for the server, database and backups; Cloudflare (US) for the domain, content delivery, and photo and backup storage; Resend (US) for service emails; Sentry (US company; data in its European Union region) for error and performance records; RevenueCat (US) for subscription verification; OpenAI (US) for automatic pre-screening of profile photos; Google (US) for Android notifications, maps and place search; Apple (US) for iPhone notifications and maps; and GitHub (US) for the second copy of the database backup and the restore test. Payments through the App Store and Google Play are handled by Apple and Google under their own responsibility.

The Board has not issued an adequacy decision for these countries. Under Article 9 of the KVKK, the legal basis for the transfer abroad is appropriate safeguards, primarily the standard contracts announced by the Board; standard contracts are notified to the Authority within five business days of signature. Explicit consent is not used as the basis for these ongoing transfers. Each provider receives only the data categories needed for the service it provides.

7. Retention and account deletion

Personal data is kept as long as needed for the related processing purpose, service security, technical needs, legal obligations and legal claims. When the reason for keeping it ends, the data is deleted, destroyed or made impossible to link to a person. Periods set so far: the message content of ended connections is deleted 30 days after they end; message evidence frozen at the time of a report, 60 days after the report is reviewed; the email hash of a deleted account, after 7 days; traffic data under Law No. 5651, after 1 year. Database backups are kept for at most 30 days, and a deleted account's data may remain in backups until this period ends. Arbitrary month or year values are not used for records whose period is not yet set; those records are deleted when their purpose ends.

When an account is deleted, direct identity and contact values such as name, email and phone are removed from the account record; profile content, match distance preference, photos and photo processing details, coffee preferences, favorite spots, personal check-ins and device notification tokens are cleared. The main content of messages you sent is changed to a deleted-message state; the last-message preview and sender details are cleared.

When an account is deleted, its email address is removed from the account. To prevent the same address from repeatedly opening and closing accounts, the address itself is not kept; only a hash of it, produced with a secret key kept on the server and impossible to turn back into the address, is kept in a separate record for 7 days, and the record is deleted when the period ends. The legal ground is proportionate legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

Safety and moderation evidence, the integrity of other users' match and chat history, candidate actions, limited product analytics, invalidated sessions, subscription and financial records and technical transaction records may be kept, linked to the internal user number, as long as their reasons continue. These records are not fully anonymous; they are pseudonymous records stripped of direct contact details, with limited access. A detailed description of account deletion is on the Privacy Policy and Account Deletion pages.

8. Your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
  • Know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom personal data is transferred,
  • Ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected,
  • Ask for personal data to be deleted or destroyed when the legal conditions are met,
  • Ask for correction, deletion or destruction to be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  • Object to a result against you that arises only from analysis by automated systems,
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

9. How to make a KVKK request

Under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, you can make your request in Turkish in these ways: by email from the email address registered on your Coff account to kvkk@coffcircle.com; by sending a request signed with a secure electronic signature or mobile signature to the same address; or in writing. For a written request, you can ask for our postal address at kvkk@coffcircle.com.

Under the Communiqué, the request must include your first name and last name, your signature (for written requests), your Turkish ID number if you are a Turkish citizen, or your nationality and passport number or, if any, ID number if you are a foreigner, your residential or business address for notifications, your email address and phone number if any, and the subject of your request. To verify account ownership, we may ask for proportionate additional information depending on the nature of the request.

Requests are concluded as soon as possible according to their nature and within 30 days at the latest, free of charge as a rule. If your request is rejected, you find the answer insufficient, or it is not answered in time, you can file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning the answer and in any case within 60 days of your request.

10. Notice versus explicit consent

This document is a privacy notice; it does not ask the user for “acceptance”, “approval” or explicit consent. If explicit consent becomes legally required for a separate processing activity, a separate process based on free will is offered for that activity.
