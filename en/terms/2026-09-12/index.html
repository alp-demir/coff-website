Terms of Use

By using Coff, you agree to follow these terms and the basic rules that keep the community safe.

This is the previous version of the Terms of Use, dated September 12, 2026. For the current version, see the Terms of Use.

1. Purpose of the service

Coff is a social coffee app for iOS and Android that makes it easier to meet new people through coffee taste, coffee spots, check-ins and social preferences. Coff aims to offer a safe and respectful social experience.

2. Age requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to use Coff. Giving a false age may lead to your account being restricted or closed.

3. User responsibilities

  • You must create your account with accurate information that belongs to you.
  • You must not impersonate another person or create a misleading profile.
  • You must respect other users' boundaries, privacy and safety.
  • You are responsible for keeping your account secure and protecting your password.

4. Prohibited behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual abuse, spam, fraud, impersonation, unauthorized advertising, calls to violence, sharing someone else's private information and misusing the platform are prohibited.

5. User content

You are responsible for your profile texts, photos, messages, check-ins and anything else you share. Coff may remove content that is inappropriate, misleading or puts safety at risk, limit its visibility or take action on your account.

6. Profile photos and moderation

Profile photos may be reviewed for safety and quality. Photos that are inappropriate, fake, belong to someone else or are misleading may be rejected. If needed, the account may be suspended.

7. Reporting, blocking and suspension

Users can report or block other users. Coff reserves the right to review reports, remove content, limit visibility, and freeze, suspend or close accounts.

8. Location and check-in features

Nearby coffee spots and check-in features depend on location data, map providers and user preferences. Place information may not always be complete or up to date. When you meet someone in real life, look after your own safety, prefer public places and report anything that makes you uncomfortable.

9. Changes to the service

Coff's features, rules or access may change over time. The service may be interrupted temporarily for maintenance, security or technical reasons.

10. Account deletion

You can delete your account inside the app. Deletion cannot be undone. Some limited records may be kept for a reasonable period for safety, abuse or legal reasons.

11. Contact

For questions about these terms, write to destek@coffcircle.com.
