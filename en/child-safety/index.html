Child safety standards

Coff has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page explains our standards, how to report, and how we act on violations.

Coff is for adults only

Only people aged 18 and over can sign up for Coff. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up, and people under 18 cannot open an account. If you think a user is under 18, you can report them inside the app with the reason “Under 18”; these reports are reviewed with priority, and confirmed accounts are closed.

Prohibited content and behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on Coff and violate the Terms of Use:

  • Sharing, requesting or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or directing others to it.
  • Any content, message or profile information that sexualizes a child.
  • Trying to contact children for sexual purposes, deceiving children, or trying to gain their trust for abuse (grooming).
  • Content that promotes, praises or facilitates the sexual abuse, exploitation or trafficking of children.

Prevention and moderation

  • Profile photos go through moderation before they go live; photos that look suspicious are sent to human review and are not shown to other users until approved.
  • Users can report profiles and chats inside the app, and block anyone they want.
  • Reports are reviewed by our management team; reports about child safety get priority.

How to report inside the app

Use Report from the menu on a Discover card, on a profile or in a chat, and choose the right reason (for example “Under 18” or “Inappropriate content”). If needed, you can block the person from the same menu.

Reporting outside the app

You can send any concern about child safety to destek@coffcircle.com with the subject “Child safety report”. Please do not attach files that contain CSAM to the email; instead, write the profile's name, the time and the context.

If you think a child is in immediate danger, call 112 first (the emergency number in Turkey). You can also report illegal content through İhbar Web, Turkey's official hotline, or to the cybercrime units of the Turkish National Police. Outside Turkey, contact your local emergency number and authorities.

What we do when there is a violation

  • We remove the content and close the account permanently.
  • Within our legal obligations, we preserve the related records and share them with the competent law enforcement and judicial authorities.
  • We report detected CSAM to the competent authorities in Turkey and, when needed, to international reporting organizations (for example NCMEC).
  • We cooperate with and respond to legal requests from the authorities.

Child safety point of contact

For questions about Coff's child safety practices and its compliance with these standards: destek@coffcircle.com
