We are here when you need help.

You can reach the Coff Circle team about your account, sign-in, profile, photo verification, safety or feedback.

Support email

For the fastest help, share the email address you use on your account and a short description of the problem.

destek@coffcircle.com

How do I report a problem?

Write to the support address about a bug in the app, a sign-in problem, a profile update issue or a delay in photo verification. A screenshot, the error message and the step where the problem happened help us solve it faster.

Reporting safety issues or abuse

If a user is bothering you, use the report and block tools inside the app. If there is a safety risk, you can also write to the support address. When there is a report, an authorized team member may review limited evidence related to that incident.

Help with your account, sign-in and profile

  • If you cannot sign in, share your email address and the error message you see.
  • If you cannot update your profile, tell us which field shows an error.
  • For photo verification, include when you uploaded the photo and your profile email.
  • For notification or location permission problems, add your device model and your iOS or Android version.

Help with account deletion

If you want to delete your account, follow the steps on the Account Deletion page. If you cannot open the app, write to the support address from the email you use on your account.
