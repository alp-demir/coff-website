Account management

Deleting your Coff Circle account

You can delete your account inside the app. Deletion cannot be undone, and for security it asks for your password.

Deleting inside the app

In the Coff Circle app, go to your profile screen, open the profile menu at the top right, go to Account security and choose Delete account. To continue, you need to tick the confirmation box and enter your current password.

If you cannot open the app

If you cannot sign in, or have trouble with the deletion step, write to destek@coffcircle.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Account deletion request”.

Data that is removed when your account is deleted

  • The real values of your account details, such as first name, last name, email and phone, are removed from the system.
  • Your profile details, bio, interests, discovery distance, coffee preferences and favorite coffee spots are cleared.
  • Your profile photo and its storage and processing details are removed.
  • Your personal check-ins and device notification tokens are deleted.
  • The content of messages you sent, the last-message previews in chat lists and your sender details are cleared.

Information that may be kept for a limited time

Some limited records of a deleted account that do not contain its direct contact details may be kept for as long as they are needed for these purposes: safety and abuse prevention, protecting other users' connection and chat history, evaluating product and connection performance, keeping sessions invalid, and verifying legal, financial or subscription transactions. These include report and block records, limited moderation evidence, relationship and usage records, and necessary technical records. Under Turkey's Law No. 5651, traffic data (from which IP address and when you connected) is kept for 1 year, and database backups for at most 30 days. Because these records can stay linked to an internal record number, they are not fully anonymous, and they do not mean live access to chats. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

If you have an active subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your Coff Premium subscription by itself. Cancel it separately in your App Store settings on iPhone, or in Google Play on Android.

Freezing instead

If you do not want to delete your account permanently, you can freeze it in the same Account security section. A frozen account is invisible; it can become active again when you sign back in.
